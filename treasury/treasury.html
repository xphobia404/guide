TREASURY MODULE

Treasury User Guide

The Treasury module is used to manage approved payment requests, prepare payment instructions, perform treasury approval, and release payments in accordance with the company payment workflow.

Payment List Review transactions that are ready or pending for payment.
Payment Process Prepare payment instructions and validate payment details.
Approval Approve payment instructions before funds are released.
Payment Release Release approved payments and record payment references.
01

Payment List

Payment List displays transactions that have completed the required Operational and Controller stages and are ready to be reviewed by Treasury.

Key Functions

  • View payment requests based on their current payment status.
  • Search transactions by vendor, payment reference, document number, or period.
  • Review payment amount, currency, source transaction, and bank details.
  • Identify urgent or overdue payments that require immediate action.

How to use Payment List

  1. Open Treasury → Payment List.
  2. Use available filters such as payment date, vendor, entity, currency, or status.
  3. Select a transaction from the list to open its details.
  4. Review the payment request, supporting documents, and approval history.
  5. Continue the transaction to Payment Process when the payment data is complete.

02

Payment Process

Payment Process is used to prepare and validate payment instructions before they are submitted for Treasury approval.

Information to validate

  • Payment date and payment reference number.
  • Vendor or beneficiary name.
  • Destination bank, account holder name, and bank account number.
  • Payment amount, currency, exchange rate, and bank charges if applicable.
  • Source transaction, invoice, Purchase Order, and supporting documents.

How to process a payment

  1. Open Treasury → Payment Process.
  2. Select a transaction that is ready for payment preparation.
  3. Review the beneficiary details and the destination bank account.
  4. Verify the payment amount, currency, taxes, deductions, and net payable amount.
  5. Enter or confirm the intended payment date.
  6. Add payment notes or references when required.
  7. Save the payment instruction or submit it for Treasury approval.

03

Approval

The Treasury Approval menu is used by authorized approvers to review and approve payment instructions before the payment can be released.

Approval checklist

  • The source transaction has completed the required workflow and approvals.
  • The invoice and payment supporting documents are valid and complete.
  • The beneficiary bank account details have been verified.
  • The payment amount, taxes, and currency are correct.
  • The payment date and cash availability are aligned with company policy.

How to approve a payment

  1. Open Treasury → Approval.
  2. Select a payment instruction with a pending approval status.
  3. Open the payment details and review all relevant information.
  4. Review the attached documents and approval history.
  5. Click Approve if the payment is valid and ready to be released.
  6. Click Return if additional data or documents are required.
  7. Click Reject if the payment should not proceed.
  8. Provide a clear note for every Return or Reject action.

04

Payment Release

Payment Release is used to release payments that have completed all required Treasury approvals and are ready to be sent through the company banking process.

How to release a payment

  1. Open Treasury → Payment Release.
  2. Select a payment that has an approved and ready-to-release status.
  3. Review the payment reference, beneficiary, bank account, amount, and currency one final time.
  4. Confirm that all approvals and supporting documents are complete.
  5. Perform the Release action according to the company procedure.
  6. Enter the bank reference number, transaction number, or payment proof if available.
  7. Save the completed payment release record.

Payment Status

Payment statuses help Treasury users understand the current position of each transaction in the payment workflow.

Status Description Recommended Treasury Action
Ready for Payment The transaction has completed prior checks and is ready for Treasury review. Review the transaction in Payment List and proceed to Payment Process when complete.
On Process The payment instruction is being prepared or requires additional validation. Complete payment details, supporting documents, or clarification before submission.
Approved The payment instruction has been approved by the authorized Treasury approver. Proceed to Payment Release according to the approved payment date.
Returned / Rejected The payment was returned for correction or rejected during the payment workflow. Review the approver note, correct the issue, and resubmit if applicable.
Released / Paid The payment has been released and recorded as completed. Confirm the bank reference and review the completed record in Transaction History.
→

Treasury Workflow

The following is the standard high-level flow for a payment managed by the Treasury module.

1
Payment List Review approved transactions that are ready for payment.
2
Payment Process Prepare and validate the payment instruction details.
3
Treasury Approval Obtain authorization based on the applicable approval matrix.
4
Payment Release Release the payment and record the bank transaction reference.
i

Workflow stages, approval levels, and available actions may differ depending on payment type, payment value, entity, currency, bank requirements, and system role permissions.
