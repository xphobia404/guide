CONTROLLER MODULE

Controller User Guide

The Controller module is used to validate transaction data, review supporting documents, confirm invoices, verify operational requests, and approve or return transactions before they proceed to Treasury.

Document Validation Review transaction completeness and supporting documents.
Invoice Confirmation Validate vendor invoices against Purchase Orders and transaction data.
Cost Control Review Payroll, Cash Advance, Allowance, and Liquidation requests.
Approval Decision Approve, return, or reject transactions based on validation results.
01

Outstanding PO

Outstanding PO is used to monitor Purchase Orders that are still open, incomplete, pending an invoice, pending payment, or requiring follow-up.

Key Functions

  • View Purchase Orders that have not completed the full workflow.
  • Review PO status by vendor, period, entity, vessel, amount, or workflow status.
  • Identify Purchase Orders that have no invoice or no completed payment process.
  • Follow up on Purchase Orders that require documents, corrections, or action from other teams.

How to use Outstanding PO

  1. Open Controller → Outstanding PO.
  2. Use available filters such as date range, vendor, entity, vessel, status, or PO number.
  3. Select a Purchase Order from the list.
  4. Review the PO details, total amount, related invoice status, and supporting documents.
  5. Follow up on incomplete or overdue Purchase Orders based on the transaction status.

02

Invoice Confirmation

Invoice Confirmation is used to validate vendor invoices before they proceed to the payment workflow.

Information to verify

  • Invoice number and invoice date.
  • Vendor identity and vendor tax information.
  • Purchase Order reference or related source transaction.
  • Invoice amount, taxes, currency, deductions, and total payable amount.
  • Invoice document, tax invoice, delivery order, receipt, or other supporting documents.

How to confirm an invoice

  1. Open Controller → Invoice Confirmation.
  2. Select an invoice with a pending confirmation status.
  3. Open the invoice detail and review all invoice information.
  4. Compare the invoice against the related Purchase Order and supporting documents.
  5. Confirm the vendor, amount, currency, taxes, and document completeness.
  6. Click Confirm if the invoice is valid.
  7. Click Return or Reject if there is a discrepancy.
  8. Provide a clear note when returning or rejecting an invoice.

03

Vendor

The Vendor menu allows Controller users to review vendor details used in Purchase Orders, invoice confirmation, and payment transactions.

Vendor information to review

  • Vendor name and vendor status.
  • Vendor code and contact information.
  • Tax identification and tax-related data, where applicable.
  • Registered bank account information.
  • Supporting legal documents or vendor documentation, if available.

How to review vendor data

  1. Open Controller → Vendor.
  2. Search for the vendor using its name, vendor code, or status.
  3. Open the vendor detail page.
  4. Compare the vendor information against the invoice or Purchase Order.
  5. Request a Master Data update if vendor information is incorrect or incomplete.

04

Payroll

The Controller Payroll menu is used to review payroll requests before they are approved and forwarded to the next workflow stage.

Payroll review checklist

  • The payroll period is correct and not duplicated.
  • Recipient information and payment components are complete.
  • The total amount matches supporting payroll documentation.
  • Entity, department, and budget references are valid.
  • Required documents are attached according to internal procedures.

How to review Payroll

  1. Open Controller → Payroll.
  2. Select a payroll record waiting for review.
  3. Review the period, recipients, payment components, totals, and supporting documents.
  4. Confirm that the data meets applicable finance and payroll requirements.
  5. Approve the record if valid, or return it for correction if information is incomplete or incorrect.

Cash Advance

The Controller Cash Advance menu is used to validate operational cash advance requests before they proceed to approval or payment processing.

Cash Advance review checklist

  • The business purpose is clearly described.
  • The requested amount is reasonable and aligned with business needs.
  • The entity, department, vessel, and budget account are correct.
  • Required quotation, estimate, or supporting documents are attached.
  • The requester has no unresolved previous Cash Advance, if that rule applies.

How to review Cash Advance

  1. Open Controller → Cash Advance.
  2. Select a Cash Advance request with a pending review status.
  3. Review the purpose, requested amount, currency, references, and attachments.
  4. Confirm that the request meets the applicable company policy.
  5. Click Verify or Approve if valid.
  6. Click Return if the request requires clarification or correction.

Liquidation

The Controller Liquidation menu is used to validate Cash Advance accountability records based on actual expense details and supporting payment documents.

Liquidation review checklist

  • The Cash Advance reference matches the liquidation record.
  • All actual expense details have been entered.
  • Invoices, receipts, or payment evidence are attached.
  • The Cash Advance amount and total actual expenses have been reconciled.
  • Any balance, refund, or additional reimbursement is clearly explained.

How to review Liquidation

  1. Open Controller → Liquidation.
  2. Select a liquidation record that is waiting for review.
  3. Review the linked Cash Advance and the actual expense breakdown.
  4. Open and validate each supporting document.
  5. Review the difference between the advance amount and actual expenses.
  6. Approve the liquidation if complete and valid.
  7. Return the record with clear notes if information or documents are missing.

08

Upload Add Docs

Upload Add Docs is used to add supporting documents to a transaction during the Controller review process.

How to upload additional documents

  1. Open the relevant transaction from the appropriate Controller menu.
  2. Select Upload Add Docs or the equivalent document upload action.
  3. Click Choose File or Browse.
  4. Select the required document from your device.
  5. Add a document description if the system provides that field.
  6. Click Upload or Save.
  7. Confirm that the uploaded document appears in the transaction attachment list.

Recommended document naming

  • invoice-vendor-name.pdf
  • tax-invoice-PO-001.pdf
  • payment-proof-reference-001.pdf
  • supporting-document-cash-advance-001.pdf

Approval

The Controller Approval menu displays verified transactions that require an approval decision before proceeding to Treasury.

How to approve a transaction

  1. Open Controller → Approval.
  2. Select a transaction with a pending approval status.
  3. Open the transaction detail and review the transaction information.
  4. Review the verification results, amount, references, and supporting documents.
  5. Click Approve if the transaction meets all requirements.
  6. Click Return if additional correction or documents are required.
  7. Click Reject if the transaction should not proceed.
  8. Provide a detailed note for every Return or Reject action.

Controller Status Reference

The available status names may vary by transaction type, but the following describes common Controller-related workflow statuses.

Status Description Recommended Controller Action
Pending Review The transaction has entered the Controller queue and requires validation. Open the detail page and review transaction information, amount, references, and supporting documents.
On Process The transaction is being reviewed or requires additional documents or clarification. Complete the validation or request additional data from the relevant user or team.
Returned / Rejected The transaction has been returned for correction or rejected due to an issue. Ensure that the reason and corrective action are clearly documented in the transaction notes.
Verified / Approved The transaction has passed Controller validation and approval. Monitor the next stage in Treasury or Transaction History.

Controller Workflow

The Controller workflow ensures that finance transactions are complete, accurate, and properly documented before they move to Treasury.

1
Receive Transaction Operational requests enter the Controller review queue.
2
Validate Data Review transaction details, amount, vendor, and references.
3
Review Documents Confirm invoices, receipts, tax documents, and other attachments.
4
Approve or Return Approve valid transactions or return incomplete records for correction.
i

The exact workflow, approval level, and available actions can differ depending on the transaction type, amount, entity, department, vessel, and configured role permissions.
