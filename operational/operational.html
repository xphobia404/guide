OPERATIONAL MODULE

Operational User Guide

The Operational module is used to create, complete, and submit operational finance transactions before they move through Controller review, approval, and Treasury payment processing.

Purchase OrderCreate procurement documents for goods and services.
Expense RequestsManage Payroll, Cash Advance, and Allowance requests.
LiquidationRecord actual expense accountability for operational funds.
ApprovalReview and act on transactions based on the approval workflow.
01

Purpose

02

Outstanding Payment

Outstanding Payment is used to monitor transaction requests or invoices that have not yet been fully processed or paid.

Key Functions

  • View transactions that are still unpaid or pending further action.
  • Search by document number, vendor, reference number, or period.
  • Monitor transaction status from submission through payment processing.
  • Identify transactions delayed by incomplete documents or pending approvals.

How to use Outstanding Payment

  1. Open Operational → Outstanding Payment.
  2. Use filters such as date, status, vendor, or document reference.
  3. Select a transaction to view its details.
  4. Review the current status, payment amount, attachments, and notes.
  5. Provide corrections or upload additional documents if the transaction was returned.

03

Payroll

Payroll is used to submit salary-related, honorarium, incentive, or other payroll payment requests.

How to create a Payroll request

  1. Open Operational → Payroll.
  2. Click Add Payroll.
  3. Select the payroll period.
  4. Enter recipient information, payment components, and payment amounts.
  5. Add descriptions and attach supporting documents when required.
  6. Review the total amount before saving.
  7. Click Submit to send the request for Controller review.

04

Cash Advance

Cash Advance is used to request operational funds in advance before actual expenses are incurred.

How to create a Cash Advance request

  1. Open Operational → Cash Advance.
  2. Click Add Cash Advance.
  3. Enter the business purpose and operational requirement description.
  4. Enter the requested amount, currency, and required funding date.
  5. Select the department, entity, vessel, or budget account if applicable.
  6. Upload supporting documents, such as quotations or expense estimates.
  7. Save as draft or click Submit to send the request for review.

!

Cash Advances that have been paid normally require accountability through the Liquidation menu. Keep all receipts and proof of expenses for the liquidation process.

05

Allowance

Allowance is used to request allowances or operational support costs based on company policy and business requirements.

How to create an Allowance request

  1. Open Operational → Allowance.
  2. Click Add Allowance.
  3. Select or enter the allowance recipient.
  4. Specify the allowance type, period, and requested amount.
  5. Add the department, entity, vessel, or cost reference if applicable.
  6. Upload supporting documents if required.
  7. Click Submit to send the request into the workflow.

06

Liquidation

Liquidation is used to record actual expenses and account for funds received through Cash Advance or another operational advance process.

How to create a Liquidation

  1. Open Operational → Liquidation.
  2. Click Add Liquidation.
  3. Select the related Cash Advance reference.
  4. Enter actual expense details and amounts.
  5. Upload invoices, receipts, bank transfer proofs, or other supporting documents.
  6. Review the difference between the Cash Advance amount and actual expenses.
  7. Provide an explanation if there is any remaining balance or additional reimbursement.
  8. Click Submit to send the liquidation to Controller for review.

07

Approval

The Operational Approval menu displays transactions that require an approval decision from a user with the appropriate approval role.

How to approve an Operational transaction

  1. Open Operational → Approval.
  2. Select a transaction with a pending approval status.
  3. Open the transaction detail and review all available information.
  4. Check the amount, vendor, description, references, and attached documents.
  5. Click Approve if the transaction is valid.
  6. Click Return if corrections or additional documents are required.
  7. Click Reject if the transaction should not continue.
  8. Provide a clear note for Return or Reject actions.

Transaction Status Reference

Status labels help Operational users understand a transaction’s current position and identify which action is required.

StatusDescriptionRecommended Operational Action
DraftThe transaction has been saved but has not been submitted into the workflow.Complete or correct the information, then submit the transaction.
SubmittedThe transaction has been submitted and is waiting for review or approval.Monitor the status and wait for the next workflow action.
Returned / RejectedThe transaction was returned for correction or rejected based on workflow review.Read the reviewer note, correct the issue, and resubmit when allowed.
ApprovedThe transaction has been approved and can proceed to the next workflow stage.Monitor the payment process through Outstanding Payment or Transaction History.
